**Statistics**

1. **26%** — Structured interviews predict job performance with 26% accuracy, tied with general cognitive ability tests as the second-best predictor of on-the-job success, behind only work-sample tests (29%). Source: Laszlo Bock, as cited by ERE. Publication: ERE. "Why Structured Interviews Are the Best Way to Hire." Year: not dated (legacy post). URL: https://www.ere.net/why-structured-interviews-are-the-best-way-to-hire/

2. **2×** — Structured interviews are twice as predictive of job performance as unstructured interviews because they minimize subjectivity and focus on job-relevant criteria. Source: research cited in SHRM Vendor Directory. Publication: SHRM (vendor directory page). "The Definitive Guide to Structured Interviewing." Year: not dated. URL: https://vendordirectory.shrm.org/company/354019/whitepapers/11772/the-definitive-guide-to-structured-interviewing

3. **78%** — Of organizations that use pre-employment assessments, 78% say those assessments have improved their quality of hire. Source: SHRM. Publication: SHRM. "2024 Talent Trends Report." Year: 2024. URL: https://www.shrm.org/content/dam/en/shrm/research/2024-talent-trends-research-overall-findings.pdf

4. **45%** — When organizations were asked what would happen if they reduced time-to-fill by just five days, 45% said quality of hire would improve. Source: Aptitude Research. Publication: Aptitude Research. "The Frontline Hiring Reckoning: A New Conversation." Year: 2025. URL: https://www.aptituderesearch.com/the-frontline-hiring-reckoning-a-new-conversation/

5. **8–9 days** — Screening and interviewing alone average 8–9 days each within the overall hiring process. Source: SHRM. Publication: SHRM. "SHRM Releases 2025 Benchmarking Reports." Year: 2025. URL: https://www.shrm.org/about/press-room/shrm-releases-2025-benchmarking-reports--how-does-your-organizat

6. **30%** — The U.S. Department of Labor estimated the cost of a bad hire can be as high as 30% of the employee's first-year wages. Source: U.S. Department of Labor, as cited by SHRM. Publication: SHRM. "Eliminating Biases in Hiring: Structured Interviewing and AI Solutions." Year: 2023. URL: https://www.shrm.org/labs/resources/eliminating-biases-in-hiring--structured-interviewing-and-ai-solutions

7. **54%** — 54% of organizations use pre-employment assessments (including structured screening) to gauge applicants' knowledge, skills, and abilities during hiring. Source: SHRM. Publication: SHRM. "2024 Talent Trends Report." Year: 2024. URL: https://www.shrm.org/content/dam/en/shrm/research/2024-talent-trends-research-overall-findings.pdf
