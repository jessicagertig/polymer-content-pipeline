# how-much-does-an-ats-cost opening rewrite 2026-11-11T13:25

## Before

<p>Most ATS cost guides are written by people who have no price to disclose. They compare vendors, estimate ranges, and hedge everything — because they have nothing at stake. The result is a category of content that gives you ballpark figures, affiliate rankings, and a lot of "it depends" — but rarely the actual numbers your budget decision requires.</p>

<p>An applicant tracking system is software that collects job applications in one place and manages candidates through each stage of a hiring process, from application to hire. Teams use it to publish jobs, review candidates, collaborate on decisions, and communicate with applicants from a single platform. The pricing, though, varies by an order of magnitude depending on the vendor, the model, and what you count as "included."</p>

<p>This article covers the four pricing models ATS vendors use, what every major platform actually charges, the hidden costs that change the number after you sign, how to calculate a realistic annual total before you commit, and what to do when you're ready to decide.</p>



## Draft

<p>Every ATS pricing guide you'll find online is written by someone who won't tell you their own price. Review sites collect affiliate fees. Analyst firms aggregate vendor-submitted data. The vendors themselves hide behind a "contact sales" button. If you're trying to figure out what an applicant tracking system will actually cost your company before you sit through a demo, you're working with estimates dressed up as facts.</p>

<p>An applicant tracking system is software that collects job applications in one place and manages candidates through each stage of your hiring process, from application to hire. Instead of tracking applicants across inboxes and spreadsheets, your team uses it to publish jobs, review candidates, collaborate on decisions, and communicate with applicants from a single platform. The price for that — depending on the vendor, the tier, and what your team actually needs — ranges from $75 a month to tens of thousands of dollars a year.</p>

<p>This guide publishes real prices from every major vendor, explains the four pricing models you'll encounter and what each one costs in practice, details the hidden fees that change the actual number, and closes with what to do before you sign anything.</p>
