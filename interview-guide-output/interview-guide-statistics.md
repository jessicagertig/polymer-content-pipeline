Source and publication: ERE, citing Laszlo Bock / Google re:Work. "Why Structured Interviews Are the Best Way to Hire," ERE, 2017. https://www.ere.net/why-structured-interviews-are-the-best-way-to-hire/

"11 Must-Ask Behavioral Interview Questions," Glassdoor, 2025. https://www.glassdoor.com/blog/11-must-ask-behavioral-interview-questions/

3. **78%** — Share of organizations that report pre-employment assessments used during the interview process have improved quality of hire. Source and publication: SHRM. "2024 Talent Trends Report," SHRM, 2024. https://www.shrm.org/content/dam/en/shrm/research/2024-talent-trends-research-overall-findings.pdf

4. **75%** — Share of job seekers more likely to apply to a company that actively manages its employer brand; and **78%** say the overall candidate experience signals how a company values its people. 5. **68%** — Share of candidates who say a company's hiring process positively or negatively impacts their decision to accept a job offer. Source and publication: SHRM. "Interview Most Critical Part of Hiring Process, Candidates Say," SHRM. https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/news/talent-acquisition/interview-critical-part-hiring-process-candidates-say

6. **82%** — Share of recruiters who have lost a top candidate because of a poor interview experience. Source: Aptitude Research, as cited by Glassdoor. Publication: Glassdoor. "How to Improve Your Interview Process," Glassdoor, 2025. https://www.glassdoor.com/blog/improve-interview-process/

"2024 Talent Trends / Recruiter Nation benchmarking data," as cited in SHRM, 2024. https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/news/talent-acquisition/recruiters-express-optimism-for-2025

8. **7 of the top 10** — Companies with the highest-rated candidate experiences conduct structured interviews. Source and publication: ERE. "5 Research-Backed Ways to Improve Your Interviews," ERE. https://www.ere.net/articles/5-research-backed-ways-to-improve-your-interviews
