### As written

<p>An applicant tracking system and a recruiting CRM both hold candidate data and both send automated messages — so why do teams end up buying one when they needed the other? <p>An ATS manages candidates who have already applied — it tracks each one through your hiring stages, coordinates interview feedback, and handles candidate communication from application to decision. A recruiting CRM manages people who haven't applied yet: passive candidates, former applicants, event attendees, and anyone a recruiter has been warming up for a role that may not be open yet. The moment a candidate submits an application, they move from CRM territory into ATS territory.</p>

<p>This article covers what each tool does, where they diverge, what running both actually looks like, and how to decide which one — or which combination — fits where your hiring stands right now.</p>
