<h2>What Ashby Is and When It's the Right Choice</h2>
<p>Ashby is an applicant tracking system built for recruiting teams that run structured, high-volume pipelines — an all-in-one platform with an ATS, sourcing CRM, interview scheduling, and recruiting analytics, built around a team that tracks pipeline velocity, source quality, and time-to-fill across many open roles at once.</p>

<h3>Where Ashby is strongest</h3>
<p>Ashby's core strength is its analytics. The reporting suite is built directly on data from its ATS, CRM, and scheduling modules, with pre-built dashboards and a custom report builder. For a recruiting team that needs to report on pipeline health, source quality, and hiring pace, that depth of data is a real asset. Its sourcing and CRM tools let recruiters build outbound email sequences, manage talent pools, and track candidates from first contact to hire without leaving the platform. Combined with customizable interview plans and embedded e-signatures for offers and NDAs, Ashby covers the full recruiting workflow end to end.</p>
<p><strong>Choose Ashby if</strong> you have a recruiting team that needs deep analytics, outbound sourcing, and a tightly integrated recruiting stack, and your hiring volume justifies the investment.</p>

<h3>How Ashby is priced</h3>
<p>Ashby prices by total employee headcount, not by recruiter seats. <h2>Polymer: Best for Growing Teams That Want a Capable, Flexible ATS Without Recruiting-Department Complexity</h2>
<p>Polymer is an applicant tracking system and job board software built for growing teams, from a founder running early hires to a company with hiring managers and a recruiter in place. Plans are tiered by how many jobs you need open at once, and every plan includes the complete feature set and unlimited users. Plato, Polymer's AI candidate reviewer, is included on every plan.</p>

<h3>Why consider it</h3>
<p>Polymer is a complete ATS. The full feature set is there on the day you sign up, covering customizable pipelines, automated candidate messaging, AI-assisted screening, and scored team reviews, with no weeks of onboarding or manual to work through first. It handles sophisticated hiring requirements without loading teams with the workflows and infrastructure built for a dedicated recruiting department. That ease of adoption follows from the platform's intentional focus on growing teams, not from any constraint on what it can do.</p>

<h3>Key features</h3>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Hiring stage message automations and templates</strong>: Message templates with placeholders for candidate name, job title, and sender fire automatically when a candidate enters a stage. Build your candidate communication once, and it runs itself through the pipeline.</li>
  <li><strong>Plato AI screening</strong>: Plato reviews each application against the requirements in your job description, marking each criterion as clear evidence, related evidence, or no evidence, and assigns an overall fit level from Excellent to Poor. Name, location, and email are removed before scoring. Auto-reviews run the moment a candidate applies; bulk screening handles existing candidates in one go.</li>
  <li><strong>Candidate reviews and review requests</strong>: Scored, written feedback is visible to the whole hiring team. Anyone you send a review request to — a team member, an advisor, a subject-matter expert — receives a Review kit with the candidate's resume and application, without needing an account or access to the rest of your pipeline.</li>
  <li><strong>Branded job board with customizable application forms</strong>: Every account gets a branded job board at signup, on a custom domain if you want one. Each job has its own application form with standard fields, custom questions, file uploads, and knockout questions that automatically flag candidates who don't meet a hard requirement.</li>
  <li><strong>Flexible integrations and a customer API</strong>: A full customer API lets your team read candidates, move applications, and post reviews programmatically. Zapier triggers connect Polymer to your other tools, LinkedIn posting is built in, and each job's pipeline is fully customizable from stage names to ordering.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Pricing</h3>
<p>Polymer publishes its pricing on its pricing page. Plans run $124, $233, and $415 per month billed annually, or $149, $279, and $499 month to month. Every plan includes the complete feature set, unlimited users, and unlimited candidates; plans differ by how many jobs can be published at once (5, 20, or 50) and by monthly Plato credits (50, 100, or 150). You can explore Polymer, set up your hiring stages, invite your team, and preview your job board before entering a payment method. The 14-day free trial starts when you publish your first job.</p>

<h3>Where it beats Ashby</h3>
<p>Polymer's pricing is based on how many roles you have open at once, not how many people work at your company. That means your ATS cost stays tied to your actual hiring activity, not your headcount. As your company grows, adding headcount during quieter hiring periods does not raise your plan cost. Plans are published in full, so you know what you're paying before a sales conversation enters the picture.</p>

<h3>Where Ashby wins</h3>
<p>Ashby's native sourcing CRM and analytics suite, with custom report builders and pipeline dashboards built on its own recruiting data, go well beyond Polymer's scope.</p>

<h3>Best fit</h3>
<p><strong>Choose Polymer if you're a growing company that wants a capable, flexible ATS that's ready to use the day you sign up.</strong> If you have a dedicated recruiting team running outbound sourcing campaigns and need advanced pipeline analytics, Ashby is built for that work.</p>

<h2>Greenhouse: Best for Mid-to-Large Companies With a Dedicated Recruiting Team and Structured Hiring at Scale</h2>
<p>Greenhouse is an applicant tracking system built for companies with a dedicated recruiting function. Its structured hiring framework covers scorecards, interview kits, and layered permissions, and is designed to coordinate hiring across many departments, hiring managers, and open roles at once, with governance features that hold their shape as the organization grows.</p>

<h3>Why consider it</h3>
<p>Greenhouse fits companies that have outgrown lighter tools and now need governance: audit trails, role-based access across departments, and structured processes that scale. It also offers an extensive integration ecosystem and Model Context Protocol (MCP) support, making it a strong fit for a technical team that wants to wire their recruiting stack together precisely. A large organization running hiring across multiple regions with strict access controls will find that level of compliance infrastructure hard to match elsewhere.</p>

<h3>Key features</h3>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Role-based permissions and multi-department governance</strong>: Granular access controls let recruiting ops assign what each hiring manager, interviewer, and admin can see and do, with audit trails across the hiring process and ISO 42001 AI certification and SOC 2 Type II compliance.</li>
  <li><strong>Interview kits and scorecards with bias auditing</strong>: Interviewers access the candidate's resume, scorecard, and prep in one view. AI surfaces scoring suggestions and scorecard summaries, with monthly third-party audits of AI outputs for bias.</li>
  <li><strong>CRM and talent rediscovery</strong>: A prospect in the CRM converts to a candidate in the ATS without an import. AI-powered sourcing and campaign pools are available, with CRM depth varying by plan tier.</li>
  <li><strong>Reporting and real-time pipeline visibility</strong>: Built-in reporting includes pipeline visibility and benchmarks across roles and departments, with advanced reporting available on higher tiers.</li>
  <li><strong>Generative AI features</strong>: Job description generation, resume anonymization, offer forecasting, and keyword filtering span all plans, with deeper AI sourcing tools on higher tiers.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Pricing</h3>
<p>Greenhouse does not publish its prices. All three plans — Core, Plus, and Pro — are custom-quoted based on hiring volume, organizational complexity, and features. The price depends on your company's size and is set in a sales call. Implementation costs are additional and vary by plan.</p>

<h3>Where it beats Ashby</h3>
<p>Greenhouse's integration ecosystem is broader than Ashby's, with more than 500 connections available. Its governance features extend further for compliance-conscious organizations, covering audit trails, multi-department permissions, and ISO 42001 AI certification, none of which Ashby publishes equivalent compliance for. Those compliance capabilities make Greenhouse the stronger choice for enterprise compliance requirements.</p>

<h3>Where Ashby wins</h3>
<p>Ashby publishes its Foundations pricing outright. Greenhouse publishes nothing across any plan. Ashby's native analytics suite draws on its own ATS, CRM, and scheduling data, giving it reporting that is deeper and more integrated than Greenhouse's standard offering. Ashby also includes outbound email sequencing and CRM tools on every all-in-one plan, while Greenhouse's CRM depth varies by tier.</p>

<h3>Best fit</h3>
<p><strong>Choose Greenhouse if you're a mid-to-large company with a recruiting department that needs enterprise-grade governance, a broad integration ecosystem, and structured hiring at scale.</strong> For a growing team without that complexity, the platform's overhead and custom pricing process introduce requirements that simpler tools avoid.</p>
<h2>Lever: Best for Talent Acquisition Teams That Need a Combined ATS and Recruiting CRM</h2>
<p>Lever is a recruiting platform that merges an ATS and a recruiting CRM into one product, built for talent acquisition teams that run both inbound hiring and outbound sourcing from the same place. Its architecture assumes a team with sourcing specialists alongside hiring managers, where the workflow treats sourcing and hiring as a continuous process rather than separate functions.</p>

<h3>Why consider it</h3>
<p>Lever's distinguishing feature is an ATS and CRM that share a single data model from the ground up. Lever fits companies where talent acquisition runs as a proactive discipline: building pipelines, nurturing prospects, and tracking candidates through long sourcing cycles before they ever apply. The CRM is built into the product's data model, not added on top of it, so candidate history, sourcing touchpoints, and application data live in one record from first contact to hire. For recruiting teams that measure themselves on pipeline health and source effectiveness, that unified record is the platform's core value.</p>

<h3>Key features</h3>
<ul>
  <li><strong>Unified ATS and CRM data model</strong>: Candidates sourced through the CRM and candidates who apply inbound share one record, with the full history from first contact through every stage of the hiring pipeline — no import step, no duplicate records.</li>
  <li><strong>Nurture campaigns and outbound sequencing</strong>: Talent acquisition teams can build multi-touch email sequences to prospects, track open and reply rates, and move candidates from nurture into an active pipeline without duplicating records.</li>
  <li><strong>Structured interview feedback</strong>: Scorecards and feedback forms collect structured input from interviewers, with the full feedback history tied to each candidate record.</li>
  <li><strong>Reporting and analytics across the pipeline</strong>: Pre-built reports cover pipeline health, source effectiveness, and time-in-stage across active roles, with the ability to filter by team or department.</li>
  <li><strong>Generative AI features</strong>: LeverTRM includes AI-assisted job description generation, candidate matching, and sourcing recommendations to accelerate pipeline-building for recruiting teams.</li>
</ul>

<h3>Pricing</h3>
<p>Lever does not publish its prices. Plans are custom-quoted based on company size and hiring needs, with no free trial.</p>

<h3>Where it beats Ashby</h3>
<p>Lever's CRM is native to its data model from the ground up. The ATS and CRM share one record, so prospect history, sourcing notes, and pipeline activity live together without any sync or import. For talent acquisition teams where the distinction between sourcing and hiring is blurry by design, Lever's architecture handles that more naturally than Ashby's modular approach.</p>

<h3>Where Ashby wins</h3>

Ashby's analytics suite, with custom report builders and dashboards built on its own data, is more developed than Lever's standard reporting. <h3>Best fit</h3>
<p><strong>Choose Lever if your team needs a single record from first prospect touch to hired and your recruiting model is built on proactive outbound sourcing.</strong> If you're hiring reactively, posting roles and working the inbound pipeline, Lever's CRM depth is infrastructure you won't use.</p>
