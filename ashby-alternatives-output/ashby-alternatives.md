## What Ashby Is and When It's the Right Choice

Ashby is a widely used applicant tracking system, and for some companies it's the right one. Its platform is an all-in-one recruiting tool — ATS, sourcing CRM, interview scheduling, and recruiting analytics — designed around a team that tracks pipeline velocity, source quality, and time-to-fill across many open roles at once.

### Where Ashby is strongest

Ashby's core strength is its analytics. The reporting suite is built directly on data from its ATS, CRM, and scheduling modules, with pre-built dashboards and a custom report builder. For a recruiting team that needs to report on pipeline health, source quality, and hiring pace, that depth of data is a real asset. Its sourcing and CRM tools let recruiters build outbound email sequences, manage talent pools, and track candidates from first contact to hire without leaving the platform. Combined with customizable interview plans and embedded e-signatures for offers and NDAs, Ashby covers the full recruiting workflow end to end.

**Choose Ashby if** you have a recruiting team that needs deep analytics, outbound sourcing, and a tightly integrated recruiting stack — and your hiring volume justifies the investment.

### How Ashby is priced

Ashby prices by total employee headcount, not by recruiter seats. Its Foundations plan, for companies of up to 100 employees, is published on [Ashby's pricing page](https://www.ashbyhq.com/pricing) at $300 to $900 per month depending on your headcount band: $300 for 1–10 employees, $400 for 11–25, $500 for 26–50, $700 for 51–75, and $900 for 76–100. Annual contracts save 10% on this tier. Its Plus plan, for 101–1,000 employees, and its Enterprise plan are quote-only.

### What's included and what's added on

All-In-One plans include AI credits for Ashby's AI features, with [additional credits available at $500 per year for 5,000 credits](https://www.herohunt.ai/blog/ashby-pricing-2026-plans-ai-credits-and-alternatives/) for annual subscribers. Advanced Scheduling and AI Notetaker are paid add-ons. Outbound email-lookup credits are metered and capped by plan — 200 per month on Foundations. Texting costs $90 per 1,000 messages on every tier.

### Who tends to look elsewhere

Companies that look elsewhere tend to do so for a few reasons. Pricing tied to total employee headcount means your ATS cost grows with your company even when your recruiting activity doesn't. The Foundations plan reaches $900 per month for a company of 76–100 people, and Plus pricing isn't published. The platform's depth — analytics dashboards, outbound sequencing, CRM workflows — is well suited to a dedicated recruiting team but can feel like a lot of overhead for a founder doing their own hiring or a hiring manager running one open role at a time. If one of those descriptions fits your team, the tools below are worth looking at.

## 1. Polymer: Best for Growing Teams That Want a Capable, Flexible ATS Without Recruiting-Department Complexity

Polymer is an applicant tracking system and job board software built for growing teams, from a founder running early hires to a company with hiring managers and a recruiter in place. Every plan includes the complete feature set and unlimited users, with plans tiered by how many jobs you need open at once. Plato, Polymer's AI candidate reviewer, is included on every plan.

### Why consider it

Polymer gives you a complete ATS you can start using today — with everything you need to run a structured hiring process, without weeks of onboarding or a manual to consult first. The platform is capable and flexible: it handles sophisticated hiring requirements, from customizable pipelines and automated candidate messaging to AI-assisted screening and scored team reviews, without loading you down with workflows and complexity built for a much larger recruiting operation. That intentional focus is what makes it easy to adopt — not a lack of functionality.

### Key features

* **Hiring stage message automations and templates**: Message templates with placeholders for candidate name, job title, and sender fire automatically when a candidate enters a stage. Build your candidate communication once, and it runs itself through the pipeline.
* **Plato AI screening**: Plato reviews each application against the requirements in your job description — marking each criterion as clear evidence, related evidence, or no evidence — and assigns an overall fit level from Excellent to Poor. Name, location, and email are removed before scoring. Auto-reviews run the moment a candidate applies; bulk screening handles existing candidates in one go.
* **Candidate reviews and review requests**: Scored, written feedback is visible to the whole hiring team. Send a review request to anyone — a team member, an advisor, a subject-matter expert — and they receive a Review kit with the candidate's resume and application, without needing an account or access to the rest of your pipeline.
* **Branded job board with customizable application forms**: Every account gets a branded job board at signup, on a custom domain if you want one. Each job has its own application form with standard fields, custom questions, file uploads, and knockout questions that automatically flag candidates who don't meet a hard requirement.
* **Flexible integrations and a customer API**: A full customer API lets your team build on top of Polymer — reading candidates, moving applications, and posting reviews programmatically. Zapier triggers connect Polymer to your other tools, LinkedIn posting is built in, and each job's pipeline is fully customizable from stage names to ordering.

### Pricing

Polymer's plans are $124, $233, and $415 per month billed annually, or $149, $279, and $499 month to month. Every plan includes the complete feature set, unlimited users, and unlimited candidates; plans differ by how many jobs can be published at once (5, 20, or 50) and by monthly Plato credits (50, 100, or 150). You can explore Polymer, set up your hiring stages, invite your team, and preview your job board before entering a payment method. The 14-day free trial starts when you publish your first job.

### Where it beats Ashby

Polymer's pricing is based on how many roles you have open at once, not how many people work at your company. That means your ATS cost stays tied to your actual hiring activity — not your headcount. As your team grows through periods when you're not actively hiring, your bill doesn't move. Plans are published in full, so you know what you're paying before a sales conversation enters the picture.

### Where Ashby wins

Ashby's native sourcing CRM and analytics suite — with custom report builders and pipeline dashboards built on its own recruiting data — go well beyond Polymer's scope.

### Best fit

**Choose Polymer if you're a growing company that wants a capable, flexible ATS that's ready to use the day you sign up.** If you have a dedicated recruiting team running outbound sourcing campaigns and need advanced pipeline analytics, Ashby is built for that work.

## 2. Greenhouse: Best for Mid-to-Large Companies With a Dedicated Recruiting Team and Structured Hiring at Scale

Greenhouse is an applicant tracking system built for companies with a dedicated recruiting function. Its structured hiring framework — scorecards, interview kits, layered permissions — is designed to coordinate hiring across many departments, hiring managers, and open roles at once, with governance features that hold their shape as the organization grows.

### Why consider it

Greenhouse fits companies that have outgrown lighter tools and now need governance: audit trails, role-based access across departments, and structured processes that scale. Its integration ecosystem — with 500-plus connections and Model Context Protocol (MCP) support — is built for a technical team that wants to wire their recruiting stack together precisely. For a large organization running hiring across multiple regions with strict access controls, Greenhouse's compliance posture is a differentiator.

### Key features

* **Role-based permissions and multi-department governance**: Granular access controls let recruiting ops assign what each hiring manager, interviewer, and admin can see and do, with audit trails across the hiring process and ISO 42001 AI certification and SOC 2 Type II compliance.
* **Interview kits and scorecards with bias auditing**: Interviewers access the candidate's resume, scorecard, and prep in one view. AI surfaces scoring suggestions and scorecard summaries, with monthly third-party audits of AI outputs for bias.
* **CRM and talent rediscovery**: A prospect in the CRM converts to a candidate in the ATS without an import. AI-powered sourcing and campaign pools are available, with CRM depth varying by plan tier.
* **Reporting and real-time pipeline visibility**: Built-in reporting includes pipeline visibility and benchmarks across roles and departments, with advanced reporting available on higher tiers.
* **Generative AI features**: Job description generation, resume anonymization, offer forecasting, and keyword filtering span all plans, with deeper AI sourcing tools on higher tiers.

### Pricing

Greenhouse does not publish its prices. All three plans — Core, Plus, and Pro — are custom-quoted based on hiring volume, organizational complexity, and features. [According to Vendr buyer data reported by compono.com](https://www.compono.com/articles/greenhouse-pricing-buyers-guide-2026), the median annual contract is around $26,570, with a range of roughly $10,207 to $74,330. Implementation costs are additional and vary by plan.

### Where it beats Ashby

Greenhouse's integration ecosystem, at 500-plus connections, is broader than Ashby's, and its governance features — audit trails, multi-department permissions, ISO 42001 AI certification — go further for compliance-conscious organizations. The depth of enterprise-grade access control is a differentiator Ashby does not specifically match at the same level of published compliance.

### Where Ashby wins

Ashby publishes its Foundations pricing outright; Greenhouse publishes nothing across any plan. Ashby's native analytics suite — built on its own ATS, CRM, and scheduling data — is deeper and more integrated than Greenhouse's standard reporting. Ashby also includes outbound email sequencing and CRM tools on every all-in-one plan, while Greenhouse's CRM depth varies by tier.

### Best fit

**Choose Greenhouse if you're a mid-to-large company with a recruiting department that needs enterprise-grade governance, a broad integration ecosystem, and structured hiring at scale.** For a growing team without that complexity, the overhead — and the opaque pricing — will be more than you need.

## 3. Lever: Best for Talent Acquisition Teams That Need a Combined ATS and Recruiting CRM

Lever is a recruiting platform that merges an ATS and a recruiting CRM into one product, built for talent acquisition teams that run both inbound hiring and outbound sourcing from the same place. Its architecture assumes a team with sourcing specialists alongside hiring managers — and a workflow where the line between sourcing and hiring is intentionally blurry.

### Why consider it

Lever fits companies where talent acquisition runs as a proactive discipline — building pipelines, nurturing prospects, and tracking candidates through long sourcing cycles before they ever apply. The CRM is not an add-on; it is baked into the product's data model, so candidate history, sourcing touchpoints, and application data live in one record from first contact to hire. For recruiting teams that measure themselves on pipeline health and source effectiveness, that unified record is the platform's core value.

### Key features

* **Unified ATS and CRM data model**: Candidates sourced through the CRM and candidates who apply inbound share one record, with the full history from first contact through every stage of the hiring pipeline — no import step, no duplicate records.
* **Nurture campaigns and outbound sequencing**: Talent acquisition teams can build multi-touch email sequences to prospects, track open and reply rates, and move candidates from nurture into an active pipeline without duplicating records.
* **Structured interview feedback**: Scorecards and feedback forms collect structured input from interviewers, with the full feedback history tied to each candidate record.
* **Reporting and analytics across the pipeline**: Pre-built reports cover pipeline health, source effectiveness, and time-in-stage across active roles, with the ability to filter by team or department.
* **Generative AI features**: LeverTRM includes AI-assisted job description generation, candidate matching, and sourcing recommendations to accelerate pipeline-building for recruiting teams.

### Pricing

Lever does not publish its prices. Plans are custom-quoted based on company size and hiring needs. No free trial is published on Lever's site.

### Where it beats Ashby

Lever's CRM is native to its data model from the ground up — the ATS and CRM are not two modules sharing data, but one record. Prospect history, sourcing notes, and pipeline activity live together without any sync or import. For talent acquisition teams where the distinction between sourcing and hiring is blurry by design, Lever's architecture handles that more naturally than Ashby's modular approach.

### Where Ashby wins

Ashby publishes Foundations pricing; Lever publishes nothing. Ashby's analytics suite — with custom report builders and dashboards built on its own data — is more developed than Lever's standard reporting. Ashby also includes interview scheduling natively, while Lever's scheduling integrations rely more heavily on third-party connections.

### Best fit

**Choose Lever if you're a talent acquisition team that needs a single record from first prospect touch to hired, and your recruiting model is built on proactive outbound sourcing.** If you're hiring reactively — posting roles and working the inbound pipeline — Lever's CRM depth is infrastructure you won't use.

## 4. Workable: Best for Companies That Want Assessments, HR Workflows, and an ATS in One Platform

Workable is an applicant tracking system and HR platform that extends hiring into assessments, HR workflows, and onboarding — all inside one product. Its design assumes a team that wants a wide set of tools in one place and is willing to pay for capabilities beyond the core ATS.

### Why consider it

Workable fits companies that want to handle more of their HR workflow alongside hiring — from assessments and async video interviews to employee onboarding — without assembling a stack of separate tools. Its higher plans add HR modules that cover basic people management. For a team that needs some HR functionality but is not ready for a full HRIS, Workable's bundled approach reduces vendor count.

### Key features

* **HR modules for onboarding and people management**: Workable extends beyond hiring into employee onboarding, time-off tracking, and HR record management on its higher plans, making it closer to a combined ATS and HRIS than a pure hiring tool.
* **Assessments and video interviews**: Built-in cognitive and personality assessments, plus async video interviews, are available within the platform without a separate vendor or integration.
* **AI sourcing and candidate database**: An AI sourcing assistant recommends candidates from a broad database of profiles, letting recruiting teams build a pipeline beyond inbound applicants.
* **Job distribution to multiple boards**: Posts to many job boards from a single publish action, with both free and paid distribution options managed from within the platform.
* **Generative AI features**: AI-assisted job descriptions, interview question generation, and candidate summarization are built into the platform, available across plans.

### Pricing

Workable's Starter plan is published at $149 per month billed annually. Its Standard and Premier plans are quoted. Month-to-month billing is available on the Starter plan with no minimum term.

### Where it beats Ashby

Workable's HR modules — onboarding, time-off, and people records — go beyond what Ashby offers, making it a reasonable choice for a company that wants to run more of its HR workflow in the same platform as its ATS. Its assessments and built-in async video interviews are native features that Ashby does not include at the same level on its standard plans.

### Where Ashby wins

Ashby's recruiting analytics are more developed than Workable's, with a custom report builder built on its own ATS, CRM, and scheduling data. Ashby's sourcing CRM — with outbound email sequences and talent pool management — is more purpose-built for a recruiting team running proactive outbound hiring than Workable's AI sourcing feature. Ashby also prices its Foundations tier openly; Workable's higher plans are quoted.

### Best fit

**Choose Workable if you want assessments, job distribution, and basic HR features in one platform and your team is comfortable paying for capabilities you may not use every day.** If a focused, capable ATS is what you need, you'll be paying for modules you won't open.

## 5. Breezy HR: Best for Small Teams That Want a Visual ATS With Built-In Video Interviews

Breezy HR is an applicant tracking system built for small teams, with an interface designed to get a non-recruiter up and running quickly. It includes async video interviews as a native feature, alongside a visual pipeline and basic candidate communication tools — without the overhead of a mid-market platform.

### Why consider it

Breezy fits small teams that want to get hiring organized fast and that want video screening built in. Its free plan — which allows one active job — lets a team post a role and get a feel for the tool before committing. The visual drag-and-drop pipeline is straightforward for a hiring manager who doesn't live in recruiting software every day.

### Key features

* **Visual drag-and-drop pipeline**: A Kanban-style board lets the team move candidates between stages visually, with stage names and structure customizable per role — a format that is immediately legible to a non-recruiter.
* **Built-in async video interviews**: Candidates record video responses to interview questions without scheduling a live call, reviewable by the team at their own pace and without a third-party tool.
* **Automated candidate messaging**: Stage-based email and SMS automations send messages when candidates move through the pipeline, with templates for common stages.
* **Job distribution to multiple boards**: Posts to many job boards from a single submission, including free and paid boards, managed from within the platform.
* **Generative AI features**: AI-assisted job description writing and candidate summarization tools are available within the platform to speed up job setup and early-stage review.

### Pricing

Breezy HR's Bootstrap plan is free for one active job. Its Startup plan is $157 per month billed annually. Business and Pro plans are higher and quoted. Month-to-month billing is available.

### Where it beats Ashby

Breezy HR has a free plan — Ashby has none. For a very small team posting one role at a time and testing whether an ATS is worth the investment, that entry point is a meaningful practical difference. The visual Kanban pipeline is also more immediately legible to a non-recruiter than Ashby's analytics-forward interface, which is built for a team that reads pipeline reports regularly.

### Where Ashby wins

Ashby's analytics, sourcing CRM, and interview scheduling go well beyond what Breezy offers. Breezy's reporting is limited compared to Ashby's custom report builder, and Breezy has no native outbound sourcing or CRM module. For a recruiting team that needs data depth, Ashby belongs in a different category.

### Best fit

**Choose Breezy HR if you're a small team posting one or two roles at a time and you want video interviews built into the tool without paying for recruiting-department infrastructure.** As your hiring volume grows, Breezy's reporting and analytics will become a constraint.

## 6. Recruitee: Best for Collaborative Hiring Teams That Want a Pipeline-First ATS

Recruitee is an applicant tracking system built around collaborative hiring, with a shared pipeline view at its center and evaluation tools designed to get the whole hiring team involved in assessing candidates. It is part of the Tellent suite of HR products.

### Why consider it

Recruitee fits teams where hiring decisions are genuinely collective — where the hiring manager, interviewers, and other stakeholders all weigh in on candidates, and where keeping the whole group aligned is the main coordination challenge. Its pipeline is visual and shareable, and its evaluation tools are built for a team making a decision together, not a solo recruiter managing a process.

### Key features

* **Shared pipeline with team collaboration tools**: A shared pipeline view lets the whole team see where candidates stand, leave structured feedback, and vote on candidates, with every team member's input visible in one place.
* **Structured evaluation and scorecards**: Scorecards and rating criteria are set per role, so interviewers evaluate candidates against the same benchmarks across the team, making the final decision easier to explain.
* **Automated candidate communication**: Stage-based automations send emails when candidates move, with templates and scheduling tools to keep communication consistent across the team.
* **Job distribution to multiple boards**: Posts to many job boards from a single publish action, with paid distribution managed from within the platform.
* **Generative AI features**: AI-assisted job description writing and candidate screening suggestions are available within the platform to accelerate job setup and the early stages of review.

### Pricing

Recruitee's Start plan is $199 per month billed annually for up to ten jobs. Grow and Optimize plans are quoted. Month-to-month billing is available on the Start plan.

### Where it beats Ashby

Recruitee's collaborative evaluation tools — shared pipelines, candidate voting, and team-facing scorecards — are more immediately accessible to a non-recruiting hiring team than Ashby's analytics-first interface. For a company where hiring decisions are made by committee, Recruitee's pipeline-first design fits that workflow more directly than Ashby's structured recruiting approach built for a dedicated talent team.

### Where Ashby wins

Ashby's analytics and reporting capabilities are substantially more developed than Recruitee's, and its native sourcing CRM — outbound email sequences, talent pools, Chrome extension candidate capture — has no equivalent in Recruitee. For a recruiting team that tracks source quality and pipeline velocity, Ashby offers data that Recruitee does not provide.

### Best fit

**Choose Recruitee if your hiring team makes decisions collaboratively and you want a pipeline-first tool that gets non-recruiters involved without a steep learning curve.** If your team needs advanced analytics or outbound sourcing, look at Ashby or Lever instead.

## 7. JazzHR: Best for Small Businesses Hiring a Few Roles a Year on a Tight Budget

JazzHR is an applicant tracking system aimed at small businesses that want to move off spreadsheets and email without spending much. Its entry price is among the lowest of any full-pipeline ATS, and it covers the basics of posting roles, collecting applications, and moving candidates through stages.

### Why consider it

JazzHR fits small businesses that hire infrequently and want an organized way to manage applicants without the cost of a mid-market platform. Its entry plan allows unlimited open jobs and unlimited candidates, which is unusual at its price point and suits a small business that keeps several roles open but doesn't fill them quickly.

### Key features

* **Unlimited jobs and candidates on the entry plan**: JazzHR's Hero plan allows unlimited open roles and unlimited candidates from the start, so a small business that keeps several positions open doesn't pay more as the number of active roles grows.
* **Customizable hiring workflows**: Teams can rename and reorder hiring stages, set up custom application questions, and create basic stage-based automations to move candidates and send communications.
* **Offer letters and e-signatures**: Offer letters can be created and sent for e-signature directly from the platform on its higher plans, without a separate tool for that step.
* **Job distribution to multiple boards**: Posts to many job boards from a single submission, with both free and paid distribution options managed from within the platform.
* **Generative AI features**: AI-assisted job description generation is available within JazzHR, though AI screening capabilities are more limited compared to purpose-built AI review tools.

### Pricing

JazzHR's Hero plan is $75 per month. Its Plus plan is $269 per month and its Pro plan is $420 per month, all billed monthly. Annual billing is available with a discount.

### Where it beats Ashby

JazzHR's entry price is a fraction of Ashby's Foundations floor. For a very small business that hires slowly and does not need analytics, outbound CRM, or advanced scheduling, JazzHR covers the fundamentals — pipeline, communication, and job posting — without the investment Ashby requires.

### Where Ashby wins

Ashby's analytics, sourcing CRM, structured interview plans, and AI features are in a different tier from JazzHR's. JazzHR has limited AI screening compared to Ashby's AI review suite and no outbound sourcing capabilities. As a team's hiring complexity grows, JazzHR's depth becomes a constraint rather than an advantage.

### Best fit

**Choose JazzHR if you're a small business posting roles occasionally and your priority is keeping costs low while getting organized.** If your hiring is growing in volume or complexity, you'll want a tool that grows with you.

## How to Choose an Ashby Alternative

The right alternative depends on which of these factors matters most to your team:

* **Team structure**: Ashby, Greenhouse, and Lever are built for a dedicated recruiting team managing many roles. Polymer, Breezy, and JazzHR are built for teams where the hiring manager or founder runs the process.
* **Outbound sourcing and CRM**: If proactive sourcing is part of your model, Ashby, Lever, and Workable have native CRM tools built for it. The others are inbound-first. For a deeper look at how talent acquisition differs from reactive hiring, see [Talent Acquisition vs. Recruitment: What's the Difference?](/blog/talent-acquisition-vs-recruitment).
* **Analytics depth**: Ashby and Greenhouse have the deepest analytics. Polymer, Breezy, and JazzHR cover the basics. If pipeline velocity and source-quality reports are a requirement, narrow to those two.
* **Implementation tolerance**: Polymer and Breezy are set up and usable in a day. Greenhouse and Lever involve an implementation process. Ashby falls in between — Foundations is lighter, but Plus and Enterprise involve onboarding support.
* **AI screening**: Polymer's Plato and Ashby both include AI-assisted candidate review. Greenhouse and Workable have AI features with different architectures. JazzHR's AI screening is limited. For more on how an ATS handles AI and the rest of the pipeline, see [How Does an Applicant Tracking System Work?](/blog/how-does-an-applicant-tracking-system-work).
* **Pricing model**: Ashby prices by total employee headcount. Polymer prices by open jobs. Workable and JazzHR price by plan tier. Greenhouse and Lever are quote-only. The right model depends on whether your headcount or your hiring activity is the more variable number.
* **Published pricing**: Polymer, Breezy, and JazzHR publish all their prices. Ashby publishes Foundations only. Workable publishes its Starter plan. Greenhouse and Lever publish nothing.
* **Budget**: JazzHR starts at $75 per month. Polymer's published plans start at $124 per month annually. Ashby's Foundations floor is $300 per month. Greenhouse and Lever require a sales conversation before you know the number.

### What is the best Ashby alternative for a lean hiring team?

Polymer is built for lean hiring teams. Its message automations handle routine candidate communication without manual work, Plato's AI screening surfaces the strongest applicants before a human reads every resume, and review requests let you bring in advisors or subject-matter experts without giving them full platform access. The result is a structured hiring process that a small team can run without a dedicated recruiter managing the tool.

### Do growing companies actually need an enterprise ATS like Ashby?

Most growing companies do not. The features that justify Ashby — deep pipeline analytics, outbound email sequencing, a full sourcing CRM — are most valuable when a dedicated recruiting team is running structured, high-volume hiring. A founder doing early hires, or a hiring manager running a few roles at once, needs a capable ATS with a clean interface, AI-assisted screening, and team collaboration tools. That does not require the complexity or the headcount-based pricing of a platform built for a recruiting department.

### How does Ashby compare to Greenhouse?

Ashby and Greenhouse are both built for companies with a dedicated recruiting function, but they serve different needs. Ashby's analytics suite is deeper and more integrated with its own recruiting data, and Ashby publishes Foundations pricing. Greenhouse focuses more on enterprise governance — audit trails, ISO 42001 AI certification, 500-plus integrations — and is better suited to large organizations with complex access control requirements. Neither is built for a growing team without a recruiting department, and Greenhouse publishes no pricing at all.
